## 1. Compare kinetics.
There are multiple cases in which we want to compare the kinetics of multiple runs

### Case 1: Compare the kinetics of different metabolites within the same run, across multiple runs

Ex: N15 labeled leucine and C13 labeled mannitol were added to the media. We want to compare the trajectories of the alpha carbons of Alanine and AlanineN15, to understand when C. diff begins to put the nitrogen from leucine onto the carbon backbone from mannitol to form alanine.

	``` 
	cd scripts/processing/process/

	python compare_trajectories.py --nmr_run_fold ../../nmr_runs/ --output_folder ../../results/compare_trajectories/alanine --run_names cdiff_mannitol_n15_umass_012425 cdiff_mannitol_n15_umass_011025 --metabolites_to_compare Alanine AlanineN15 --plot_title "Alanine alpha carbon in mannitol"
	```

### Case 2: Compare the kinetics of the same metabolites across different conditions:
Ex: We want to compare the rate of acetate, alanine, and ethanol production in the presence of mannitol vs glucose.

	```
	cd scripts/processing/process/

	python compare_trajectories_across_conditions.py --nmr_run_folder ../../nmr_runs/ --output_folder ../../results/compare_trajectories/mannitol_v_glucose/ --datasets cdiff_glucose_13C_mgh_122420 cdiff_glucose_13C_mgh_051921 cdiff_glucose_13C_mgh_101322 cdiff_mannitol_13C_mgh_030323 cdiff_glucose_13C_umass_100424 cdiff_mannitol_13C_umass_110824 cdiff_mannitol_13C_umass_112224 --mets Alanine Acetate Ethanol

	```
